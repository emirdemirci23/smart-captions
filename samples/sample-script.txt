Hi everyone, and welcome back to the channel! Today we're going to talk about something that a lot of you have been asking about: how to make captions that actually look good.

First of all, captions should be easy to read. That means you shouldn't put too many words on the screen at the same time, but you also shouldn't flash one word after another.

Second, never split a phrase in the middle. If someone says "New York City" or "thank you so much", those words belong together.

And finally, timing matters. A caption should appear when the words are spoken, and stay on screen for at least 1 second. Let's get started!
